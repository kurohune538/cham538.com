Hey there!

I'm Shinnosuke Komiya

I'm an Art/Design Engineer / Human Computer Interaction researcher / Creative Technologist.
‍
My grandfather was a craftsman who made Buddhist altars and both of my parents are architects.
I was inspired by them and spent my childhood crafting and creating my idea a lot.
‍
While I studied product design in university, I was inspired to utilize my skills to develop something tangible and functioning as a product.
‍
This is why I have pursued to develop my knowledge and skills in engineering, design and arts.

My goal

My research theme in life is human augmentation. Human augmentation is not only about the expansion of physical capabilities like power suits, but also about the expansion of cognition and the mental.
‍
Through such human augmentation, I would like to continue to create new perspectives that overturn common sense and change cognitions to solve complex social problems.

For this reason, I have recently been interested in exploring and experimenting with XR, a technology that can intentionally cause errors in cognition.
